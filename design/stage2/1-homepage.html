Measure at home

Measuring takes about five minutes.

All you need is a steel tape measure. Measure to the nearest 1/8 inch, and we double-check every size before anything is made.

Read the full guide
1
Choose inside or outside mount
Inside sits within the window frame. Outside covers the frame from the wall.
2
Measure the width in three places
Top, middle and bottom. For inside mount, use the smallest number.
3
Measure the height in three places
Left, center and right. For inside mount, use the largest number.
